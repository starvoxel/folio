# PRD area A: Reporting

Part of [Month-End Spending Review](month-end-review.md). Reporting was called Analysis and trends in the first draft; the nav item was Trends. Version 1 looks back: each month vs the previous month and vs a recent average. Target budgets are version 2.

| ID    | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Priority |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| A1.1  | Month overview shows totals, spending by category, notes and review status, with this month vs last month and vs average                                                                                                                                                          | must     |
| A1.2  | Money in vs out shows income, spending and savings per month over 6 to 12 months, with the net result per month                                                                                                                                                                   | must     |
| A1.3  | Category over months shows one category's spending across months at parent or sub level, each month vs the category's average                                                                                                                                                     | must     |
| A1.4  | Month vs average lists every category this month against its rolling average, biggest increases and decreases first                                                                                                                                                               | must     |
| A1.5  | Transactions is a searchable list filtered by month, account, category and status                                                                                                                                                                                                 | must     |
| A1.6  | Selecting any category, bar, month or legend row in a chart, including the month breakdown, opens the transactions behind it                                                                                                                                                      | must     |
| A1.7  | Only accounts included in month-end analysis count, and confirmed transfers and transactions marked as confirmed fraud ([I](month-end-review-import.md) I1.22) are left out                                                                                                       | must     |
| A1.8  | The rolling average defaults to 6 months, switchable to 3 or 12 in each view, and counts only locked months                                                                                                                                                                       | must     |
| A1.9  | Refunds reduce spending in their own category                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | must     |
| A1.10 | Income uses an Income parent category with subcategories Salary, Government benefits, Interest and dividends, Gifts, Other; shown as household totals, never per person                                                                                                           | must     |
| A1.11 | A split transaction contributes each part to its own category                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | must     |
| A1.12 | Savings are shown apart from spending, never inside it                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | must     |
| A1.13 | With fewer locked months than the average length, the average uses what exists and the view states how many months it is based on; with none, comparisons are hidden                                                                                                              | must     |
| A1.14 | Totals for a closed month are identical every time they are shown, across all views                                                                                                                                                                                               | must     |
| A1.15 | Reporting opens on a month breakdown of the selected month by default                                                                                                                                                                                                             | must     |
| A1.16 | The breakdown shows a donut chart of spending by category, switchable between main and subcategory level; the main level lists every main category and the subcategory level shows the top nine plus "Other"; uncategorized spending is shown separately and never inside "Other" | must     |
| A1.17 | The breakdown shows a donut chart of money in by source                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | must     |
| A1.18 | The breakdown shows a bar of how the month's money in was spent, saved or left over, and flags overspend when spending plus savings exceeds money in                                                                                                                              | must     |
| A1.19 | Each donut's legend rows open the matching transactions (A1.6)                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | must     |

Flow and rules: open months show live figures marked as not final; they are never included in averages.

Assumptions (not in the write-up): A1.15 to A1.19 come from the prototype after it was updated from this PRD (overspend follows the prototype: spending plus savings above money in); A1.13 (averages with fewer locked months than the window, comparisons hidden with none) and A1.14 (closed-month totals identical in every view, the testable form of the primary success measure) are proposed; open-month figures are marked as not final.

An account archived mid-month is not included in that month's month-end checks, but its transactions still count in spending and income ([S](month-end-review-setup.md) S1.14, confirmed in review).
