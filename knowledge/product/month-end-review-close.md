# PRD area M: Month-end review and locking

Part of [Folio](month-end-review.md). Closing freezes a month's transactions, balances and notes; this is what makes the numbers trustworthy.

| ID    | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Priority |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| M1.1  | Review is an exceptions queue by default listing: new merchants, uncategorized items, unusually large amounts, suspicious-transaction flags, and suggested transfer pairs, using thresholds set at planning and adjustable in Settings                                                                                                                                                                    | must     |
| M1.2  | An optional full pass steps through every transaction in the month                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | should   |
| M1.3  | Resolving a queue item removes it; an item sent back for categorizing returns to the queue when fixed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | must     |
| M1.4  | Each month has a free-text note, editable any time and kept with the month once locked                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | must     |
| M1.5  | The close checklist shows: earlier months closed (M1.17); every included account covered through month end, or short of it by no more than N days (a warning, see M1.15); no uncategorized transactions; transfer suggestions resolved; closing balances confirmed; free-form net worth entries valued; notes (optional)                                                                                  | must     |
| M1.6  | Close is disabled while any blocking checklist item is unmet and says which; warn-and-confirm items (M1.15) do not block                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | must     |
| M1.7  | Closing locks the month: imports, rule changes and edits cannot change its transactions or totals                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | must     |
| M1.9  | Reopening lives in the month's overflow menu, not the main screen                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | must     |
| M1.10 | Reopening asks for confirmation more than once, explains what will change (such as later net worth figures), and requires a short reason kept in the month's history                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | must     |
| M1.11 | When a reopened month is closed again with changed balances, every later locked month is flagged for re-confirmation and its net worth figure is not trusted until then; reopening alone flags nothing. A flagged month shows Re-confirm, and re-confirming is recorded in its history                                                                                                                    | must     |
| M1.12 | Each month shows status: open, ready to close, closed, or closed but needing re-confirmation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | should   |
| M1.13 | A month's history lists closes, reopens with their reasons, re-confirmations, coverage-gap confirmations, and balance overrides, with dates                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | should   |
| M1.14 | Closing a month is undoable only through the guarded reopen, not Undo                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | must     |
| M1.15 | When an account that was active for the whole month has a covered-through date short of month end by N days or fewer (default 3, set in Settings), closing shows a warning naming the account and the gap and proceeds only after confirmation; a larger gap blocks closing. An account archived at any point during a month is not included for that month at all ([S](month-end-review-setup.md) S1.14) | must     |
| M1.17 | A month can be closed only when every earlier month is closed; the checklist's first item is "Earlier months are closed"                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | must     |

Closing typically happens one to three weeks after the month ends, when statements arrive. Flow and rules: "unusually large" and "new merchant" thresholds are defined at planning time. Data kept: notes, close and reopen history, confirmed balances.

Assumptions (not in the write-up): M1.15 relaxes the write-up's requirement that every account be covered through month end, per the review comment on I1.12 (the N=3 default is an assumption); per-account review is S1.20; M1.3 (queue items resolve and reappear when sent back), M1.12 and M1.13 (status labels, history list) and M1.14 (close is not undoable except by guarded reopen) are proposed; that months close in order (M1.17) is stricter than the write-up and was confirmed by Jeremy after prototyping.
